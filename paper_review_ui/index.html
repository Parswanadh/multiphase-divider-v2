Academic Theory Deep Dive

Frequency Division (Divide-by-2)

A frequency divider produces f_out = f_in/N. Divide-by-2 is achieved by toggling on every clock edge, generating quadrature/multiphase outputs.

CML D-Latch

Current-Mode Logic D-latch uses differential pair for sampling, cross-coupled pair for holding. Controlled by differential clock CK/CKb alternating between sample (CK high) and hold (CK low).

Elmore Delay Model

t_pdr = t_pdf = 4RC

Dominant pole approximation: delay = sum of resistance × capacitance along signal path.

Cascode Biasing

Cascode transistor increases output resistance and ensures proper V_DS across lower transistor, preventing saturation issues in current mirrors.

Circuit Analysis

Baseline (Kuo et al., 2025)

  • 4 cascading differential D-latches with alternating cross-connections
  • M1-M6 = 0.9µm/0.18µm, M7 = 1.8µm/0.36µm
  • R1=R2 = 1.5kΩ to VDD
  • f_in=1.4GHz → f_out=700MHz, 8 phases @ 45°
  • Power = 2.34mW, Fmax = 1.4GHz

V2 Enhanced Design

  • Regulated Cascode Tail: M7A,M7B,Mcasc ensures V_DS ≥ 180mV in saturation
  • Active PMOS Peaking Loads: Replace resistors, provide bandwidth extension, 45% area reduction
  • Dynamic Phase Interpolator: Varactor-based tuning for phase alignment
  • Performance: Fmax = 3.2GHz, Phase Error ≤ 0.5°, Power = 2.2mW
