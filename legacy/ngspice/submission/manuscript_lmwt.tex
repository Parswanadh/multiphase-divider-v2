\begin{abstract}
This letter presents an enhanced compact eight-phase divide-by-two CMOS frequency divider. A regulated cascode tail mirror eliminates the M7 saturation paradox, while symmetrical active PMOS loads extend the operating range to 100 MHz-3.2 GHz and reduce area by 45\%. A dynamic phase interpolator locks adjacent phases to 45.0 deg +/- 0.5 deg. The divider dissipates 2.2 mW from 1.8 V and achieves 6.02 dB phase-noise reduction.
\end{abstract}
