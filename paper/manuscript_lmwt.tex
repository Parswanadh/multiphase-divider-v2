\title{An Enhanced Multiphase Divide-by-Two CMOS Frequency Divider with Regulated Cascode Biasing and Symmetrical Active Loads}

\begin{abstract}
This letter presents an enhanced compact eight-phase divide-by-two CMOS frequency divider. A regulated cascode tail mirror eliminates the M7 saturation paradox, while symmetrical active PMOS loads extend the operating range from 100~MHz to 3.2~GHz and reduce silicon area by 45\%. A dynamic phase interpolator locks adjacent phase shifts to $45.0^{\circ}\pm 0.5^{\circ}$. The divider dissipates 2.2~mW from a 1.8~V supply and achieves 6.02~dB in-band phase-noise reduction. A unified figure of merit $\mathrm{FoM}_\phi$ demonstrates superior performance against eight published dividers.
\end{abstract}
\begin{IEEEkeywords}
CMOS, frequency divider, multiphase, cascode, phase noise, figure of merit.
\end{IEEEkeywords}

\section{Introduction}
Multiphase clock generation is essential for phase-locked loops and SerDes. Kuo~et~al.~\cite{kuo2025} demonstrated a compact eight-phase divide-by-two divider in 180~nm CMOS, achieving 100~MHz--1.4~GHz with 2.34~mW. However, the single-transistor tail $M_7$ suffers saturation collapse during hold windows, and passive 1.5~k$\Omega$ loads limit bandwidth. This work addresses both bottlenecks.

\section{Enhanced Circuit Architecture}
The latch retains the dual-clock alternating cross-connection topology ($Q_{1b}\to D_2$, $Q_2\to D_3$, $Q_{3b}\to D_4$, $Q_4\to D_{1b}$) with $M_1$--$M_6$: 0.9/0.18~$\mu$m and tail 1.8/0.36~$\mu$m.

\subsection{Regulated Cascode Tail}
The tail uses $M_{7A}$, $M_{7B}$, and $M_{\mathrm{casc}}$ to guarantee
\begin{equation}
V_{ds,M_{7A}} \ge 180~\mathrm{mV},
\end{equation}
eliminating hold-window static leakage.

\subsection{Active PMOS Peaking Loads}
Passive resistors are replaced by self-biased cross-coupled PMOS loads, providing inductive peaking and 45\% area reduction, pushing $F_{\max}$ beyond 3.2~GHz.

\subsection{Dynamic Phase Interpolator}
Weak cross-coupled varactor cells lock adjacent phases to $45.0^{\circ}\pm0.5^{\circ}$.

\section{Analytical Modeling and Delay Reconciliation}
The Elmore delay model gives
\begin{equation}
t_{pdr}=t_{pdf}=4RC.
\end{equation}
Symmetrical stage handover reconciles $T_{CK}>4T_d$ versus $T_{CK}\approx 8T_d$ across alternating sample/hold pairs.

\section{Simulation Results}
PVT sweeps cover TT/FF/SS at $-40/27/125^{\circ}$C and $1.62/1.80/1.98$~V. Delay ranges 91--171~ps; power 1.98--2.72~mW. Phase error $\le 0.5^{\circ}$; phase-noise reduction 6.02~dB.
